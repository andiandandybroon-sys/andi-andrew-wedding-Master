Getting there and home

Edinburgh Accies is on Raeburn Place in Stockbridge. There is no guest parking available at the venue, so public transport or a taxi is strongly recommended.

By bus

Regular buses connect Stockbridge with Edinburgh city centre. Please check the latest route and timetable before travelling.

By train

Edinburgh Waverley is the main city-centre railway station. Continue to Stockbridge by bus or taxi.

By tram

There is no tram stop beside the venue. From Edinburgh Airport, take the tram into the city centre and continue by bus or taxi.

By taxi

Taxis can drop guests close to the venue. Please arrange your journey home in advance for the approximately 1:00 AM finish.

Parking: Limited paid on-street parking may be available around Stockbridge, subject to restrictions and availability. Please check all signs carefully.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What should I wear?

Dress to impress. Tuxedos, suits, formal dresses and kilts are all very welcome. Think glamorous casino evening with a Scottish twist.

What time does the celebration finish?

The celebration is expected to finish at approximately 1:00 AM.

Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Vegetarian options will be available. Please provide details of any allergies, intolerances or other dietary requirements when completing your RSVP, including severity and any cross-contamination concerns. We will share the information with the venue, but no dish should be assumed allergen-free unless confirmed.

Can I bring a plus-one?

We are only able to accommodate the guests included on your invitation. Please enter the names of everyone attending when completing your RSVP.

Will children be attending?

Yes. Children who are included on their family’s invitation are very welcome to join the celebration.

Is parking available?

There is no guest parking at the venue. Limited paid on-street parking may be available around Stockbridge, subject to restrictions and availability. Public transport or a taxi is recommended.

Should I book a taxi home?

Yes. We recommend arranging your return journey in advance, particularly because the celebration is expected to finish at approximately 1:00 AM.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes, the venue is accessible. Please include any specific access, mobility or seating requirements when completing your RSVP.

Does the bar accept card payments?

Yes. Card and contactless payments are accepted at the bar.
